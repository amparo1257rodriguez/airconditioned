12-Volt Swampy SpaceKoolr Model S154

[Specifications]

The Swampy Model S154 is a favorite with Boaters, Rv'ers, Pet Owners, and Truckers with Sleepers. The four cool air outlets have the highest velocity of any 12v evaporative cooler on the market today. The S154 Model sits on a counter, shelf or on the floor. A fully charged 105 amp hour deep cycle battery should last from 8 to 17 hours on low or medium speeds without charging.
