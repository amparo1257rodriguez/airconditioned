[Specifications]


The Model T304 Swampy Evaporative Cooler is very popular for vans, combines, tractors, graders and other vehicles where Swampy would sit on a floor area. The T304 like the T154 has special baffling to help prevent water from being sprayed around in rougher riding vehicles. A fully charged 105 amp hour deep cycle battery should last from 8 to 17 hours on low or medium speeds without charging.
