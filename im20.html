How do the Ice Models Work?

The innovative IceMystr IM20 combines ice induced Air Conditioning with Evaporative cooling. The cold water rushes through a special Cooling Core to produce air-conditioned air instead of merely evaporative cooled air! The water contained in the IceMystr is cooled by ice or ice substitutes like reusable Freeze Packs which are available in many local stores in most areas. Another great feature is that you can always get cooler air from an IceMystr than a standard Evaporative Cooler, even after the ice melts. This is posible because of the two stage cooling process. A fully charged 105 amp hour deep cycle battery should last from 8 to 15 hours on low or medium speeds without charging.

For over 100 years different methods of blowing air through Ice always obtained disappointing results & minimal relief from the heat. Ice Chilled Water solved the mystery of how Ice may be used for cooling relief! If you are convinced blowing air into ice works Click Here.

The Model IM-20 IceMystr has a fully insulated base which holds enough ice to allow from 30 to 40 minutes of air conditioned comfort before adding more ice. Add a small amount of water and the rugged submersible pump lifts the icy water and pressures it through the "custom cooling core" then into the evaporative pad system. When the IceMystr ice supply is depleted you have cooling by evaporation which is effective in the dryer climates. Utilizing the IceMystr dual cooling system you use the ice feature for the humid times in your area or use evaporative cooling alone when the air is dry. A powerful system combining frigid ice water with evaporative cooling adds up to a great alternative to portable air conditioning. (If you need Air Conditioning over a longer period of time look into the IceStrMystr Model IM-30).

The IM-20 is baffled like the T-304, T-154 & IM-30 to keep the water from splashing out when used over rough terrain. The IM-20 evaporative system will cool for four to eight hours on one fill of water (2 gal. / 7.5 ltr.). Incorporating the dual cooling system, by adding ice, typically increases the cooling efficiency for over an hour in dryer climates. You will probably want to accelerate cooling by utilizing the reusable Freeze Packs. It is so easy to slide the cooling unit back from the insulated base, add the Freeze Packs and even your favorite beverage into the insulated base. Slide the cooling unit back, turn on the IceMystr and feel COOL!

How it works: The ice in the base chills the water. The water is then pumped into a special copper-chilling core and also through the evaporative pads. The blower motor draws ambient air through the evaporative pads pre-cooling the air before it is drawn through the chilling core. After the air is cooled it is directed outward to cool the occupants. The water that has been used and not evaporated during this process is then returned back into the base to complete the cycle. Any excess water may be easily pumped from the base by using a presurized draining tube that may be extracted and retracted into the side of the IM-20.
