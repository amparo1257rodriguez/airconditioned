[Specifications]

The Swampy T154 is our most popular evaporative cooler model and is used for all types of 12 volt applications. Four cool air outlets blow air in three directions and will sit on the seat or floor of most any vehicle. The T154, like the T304 has special baffling to help prevent water from being sprayed around in rough riding vehicles. A fully charged 105 amp hour deep cycle battery should last from 8 to 17 hours on low or medium speeds without charging.
